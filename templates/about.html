🌾 About OptiCrop

OptiCrop is an AI-powered Smart Agricultural Production Optimization Engine that recommends the most suitable crop based on soil nutrients and environmental conditions.

🎯 Project Objectives

🌱 Increase Crop Yield

Recommend crops that maximize productivity.

💧 Save Water

Encourage efficient irrigation and water usage.

🌍 Sustainable Farming

Promote eco-friendly agricultural practices.

⚙️ How It Works

1️⃣ Enter Soil Data

Provide Nitrogen, Phosphorous, Potassium, Temperature, Humidity, pH and Rainfall.

2️⃣ AI Processing

The trained Machine Learning model analyses all the parameters.

3️⃣ Crop Prediction

The system recommends the best crop for cultivation.
